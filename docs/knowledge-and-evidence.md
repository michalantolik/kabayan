# Knowledge and Evidence

## Purpose

This document separates project decisions, hypotheses, and collected evidence. Decisions about
how to learn are not market evidence.

## What we have decided

- `Pinoy Poland` remains a working name.
- The human goal is to make living and working in Poland easier for Filipinos.
- No first product problem or capability has been selected.
- Discovery Landing V1 will make discovery tangible, explain the project, invite survey
  participation, show the discovery loop, and explore naming before broader sharing.
- Public UI should represent the project's actual maturity and must not present hypothetical
  product capabilities as if they exist.
- Pilot 0 will precede the small Filipino pilot, without making one person a validation gate or
  community representative.
- The survey will be improved from pilot learning before broader sharing.
- Aggregate results, observation, and useful follow-up conversations will be used to investigate
  repeated problems and how people currently solve them.
- Evidence should determine the first problem to test, followed by a smallest-useful-solution
  experiment rather than a large platform.

These are project and process decisions. They do not show that a market need exists.

## What remains hypothetical

Potential needs may involve work, finding or changing jobs, documents, residence and work
procedures, Polish letters and public offices, accommodation, banking, taxes, healthcare,
transportation, language, contracts, family matters, trustworthy information, community,
everyday life, or problems discovery has not yet revealed.

None of these areas is a confirmed need, priority, feature, navigation section, or product
requirement. The final brand, Filipino public copy, product form, business model, and technology
also remain unresolved.

## Evidence we do not yet have

No Pilot 0 or survey pilot has occurred, and no respondent evidence has been recorded. The project
does not yet have:

- validated community demand;
- a validated first problem, feature, or problem ranking;
- product-market fit or usage evidence;
- demonstrated willingness to pay;
- a validated business model;
- evidence of employer demand;
- Pilot 0 evidence or small-pilot evidence; or
- naming validation.

A prototype will not itself supply this evidence. Positive reactions to its visuals, wording, or a
candidate name would not validate an underlying problem or solution.
