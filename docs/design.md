# Discovery Landing Design Direction

## Role of Discovery Landing V1

Discovery Landing V1 is a small, real, responsive experience implemented in `Kabayan.Web` and used
to support discovery before broader survey sharing. The committed static prototype remains its
current visual, content, interaction, and responsive reference. It should explain the project
simply, establish enough trust to invite
participation, connect to the short survey, show how discovery works, and present the chosen **Kabayan Poland** name without pretending that the product itself is validated.

It is not the first validated product capability. Public UI must reflect the project's actual
maturity: do not present jobs, documents, housing, healthcare, community, or any other hypothetical
area as an existing product section or promised capability.

## Public narrative

The current conceptual flow is:

1. Introduce **Kabayan Poland**, the human goal, English / Filipino / Polish choice, and the fact
   that the project is starting by listening; offer a clear survey action.
2. Explain that we want to understand real experiences, difficulties, what already works, and what
   could genuinely help rather than guess.
3. Introduce the approximately five-minute, privacy-conscious survey and state that sensitive
   documents and unnecessary personal information are not wanted.
4. Show the vertical discovery loop — Listening, Learning, Choosing, Building, Learning again —
   with Listening visibly current.
5. Present **Kabayan Poland** as the chosen Poland-market name. During research, ask what it made people expect before explanation and whether it carries confusing or unwanted associations, especially recruitment.
6. End with a participation invitation that connects experience to what deserves investigation.

This is direction, not frozen information architecture or final copy.

## Proposed origin story

The project has a real personal origin. Gladys is Filipino, Michał is Polish, and they are a couple
living together in Poland. Their shared everyday experience exposed a simple observation: things
that seem obvious when someone already knows Poland can be much less obvious to someone who is new
to the country.

That experience raised the discovery question behind the project: which experiences are specific
to them, and which are also experienced by other Filipinos living in Poland? This explains why the
project started. It is one experience, not evidence that the project already knows what Filipinos
need.

The public framing should be shared and modest, using **Gladys & Michał** in this context. A small
section may use a direction such as “It started with us” or “It started close to home,” but these
are framing examples rather than final copy. The page should remain primarily about Filipinos'
experiences, listening, the survey, and the discovery process—not about the couple.

Current presentation hypothesis:

- one small section of roughly two or three short paragraphs;
- no biographies, founder profiles, job titles, personal timeline, or separate About page;
- no photograph is needed for the first local prototype;
- one natural photograph together may be considered later and reviewed separately if both people
  explicitly agree before public use;
- exact placement, copy length, and visual treatment remain subject to prototype review.

Do not frame the story as Michał building something for Gladys, assign either person an invented
project role, or imply that Gladys represents Filipinos or that Michał is an expert on Filipino
experiences. Their shared experience created questions; discovery must still look beyond it.

The minimum proposed narrative may be included in a local prototype so Gladys can review the real
page rather than approve an abstract description. For that local review it may use `Gladys`,
`Michał`, `Gladys & Michał`, the Filipino-Polish relationship, living together in Poland, and the
short explanation of how shared experience raised discovery questions. Local review is not
publication approval.

Treat GitHub Pages as public unless a future deployment is demonstrably access-controlled. Do not
publish Gladys's name, photograph, relationship information, attributed words, or project role
there until she has reviewed the page and explicitly confirmed the specific elements that may be
public. Refine the story, language, and design after her review, then publish only the approved
personal elements. Confirmation should remain simple and specific rather than becoming a formal
consent system.

Keep the story truthful, brief, and privacy-conscious. “Living together in Poland” is enough for
the current direction. Do not require a city, relationship dates or timeline, addresses, employers,
travel history, interests, detailed location history, or other personal details. A later story may
be edited for brevity and privacy, but not into something materially different from reality.

## Naming and language

**Kabayan** is the project/repository identity. **Kabayan Poland** is the chosen public name for the Poland discovery market, with `kabayanpoland.com` as the intended primary domain once availability is confirmed and it is secured. The previous `Pinoy Poland` working name and the Gabay/Kasama/Kaagapay/Buhay/Tulong candidate interaction are historical discovery material, not current public UI.

Do not ask a small group to vote for a brand. Let participants encounter **Kabayan Poland** in the real page and ask, before explanation where practical, what they expected the site to be. Record useful associations, especially any assumption that it is a recruitment/employment agency.

`Kabayan` is widely used outside this project, so do not imply exclusive ownership of the generic term. Formal trademark clearance remains a separate pre-commercial task.

A user-facing language order such as `FIL · EN · PL` is a UI hypothesis, not a settled label or
order. Refine it during implementation and Pilot 0 without confusing it with editorial source
order.

## Visual and interaction character

Aim for an experience that is warm, human, trustworthy, optimistic, simple, modern, and Filipino
in character without stereotypes. Avoid making it feel primarily like a government portal,
employment agency, law office, fintech product, AI startup, or generic corporate landing page.

Current direction:

- light-first; no dark mode added merely for completeness;
- warm neutral or off-white foundation;
- trustworthy deep blue with a restrained warm yellow or gold accent;
- generous whitespace and strong readability;
- natural human photography only when suitable and truthful;
- mobile-first, responsive behavior and accessible actions;
- a vertical process that reads naturally while scrolling on a phone and remains coherent on
  larger screens.

Exact colors, typography, images, layout, animation, copy, and interaction details remain
exploratory until implementation and review.

## Design history

An earlier high-fidelity **Future Vision Concept** explored a richer possible website and helped
identify the desired visual character. It may contain hypothetical future product areas. It is not
a promise, specification, roadmap, or feature backlog. No concept asset is assumed to exist in the
repository.

**Discovery Landing V1** instead represents current reality: it explains discovery, asks for
participation, and does not advertise unbuilt capabilities. Future design discussions should keep
that distinction explicit.
