# Brand and Market Strategy

## Current identity

- **Project / repository:** `kabayan`
- **Initial public market name:** **Kabayan Poland**
- **Intended primary domain:** `kabayanpoland.com`, subject to live availability confirmation and registration
- **Current market:** Poland only
- **Current audience:** Filipinos living or working in Poland, people who previously did so, and Filipinos considering moving to Poland

`Pinoy Poland` was the initial working name. Candidate-name exploration helped clarify the direction, but the project no longer needs a public naming vote. The naming decision is deliberately reversible if real audience evidence exposes a serious problem.

## Why Kabayan Poland

The name identifies the people and geographic context without deciding what the eventual product must be. That is useful while the first problem and solution remain unknown.

Research before adoption supports treating `Kabayan` as culturally natural and recognizable in Filipino contexts. It also shows that `Kabayan` is widely used by unrelated businesses and organizations, including employment/recruitment contexts. The project must therefore avoid implying exclusive ownership of the generic word and should test whether **Kabayan Poland** is mistaken for a recruitment agency.

Ordinary web research did not reveal an obvious established exact-match `Kabayan Poland` web brand or indexed `@kabayanpoland` presence. This is useful screening evidence, not legal or registration clearance.

## What still needs direct verification

Before relying on the public identity:

1. Confirm `kabayanpoland.com` live with a registrar/RDAP-capable source and register it if available.
2. Check and, where useful, secure `@kabayanpoland` on the social platforms the project will actually use.
3. Consider `kabayanpoland.pl` as the main defensive country-domain variant.
4. Consider `kabayan-poland.com` only if inexpensive and useful as a defensive redirect.
5. Do not buy a large collection of weak variants merely because first-year prices are cheap; renewal cost matters.
6. Before meaningful commercial investment in the brand, perform formal trademark clearance for relevant classes and jurisdictions (for example Poland/EU as appropriate). Ordinary search is not legal clearance.

Defensive domains should redirect to the primary domain rather than create separate sites.

## How to test the chosen name

Do not ask a small group to vote among three names. A small popularity vote can select what sounds nicest without revealing what the name communicates.

Instead, let people encounter **Kabayan Poland** naturally, ideally through the real page/domain, then ask before explaining the project:

> When you first saw “Kabayan Poland”, what did you expect this website to be about?

Follow with neutral questions about whether the name feels natural, strange, negative, misleading, or overly associated with recruitment. Do not lead participants toward `community`, `help`, `jobs`, or another expected answer.

A good reaction to the name does not validate the underlying problem, product, or market. A serious repeated misunderstanding can justify revisiting the name.

## Poland first, other markets later

Poland is the learning market, not a template that is assumed to work everywhere.

```text
Kabayan
   |
   +-- Kabayan Poland       <- current discovery market
   |
   +-- Kabayan Germany      <- possible future experiment
   +-- Kabayan Austria      <- possible future experiment
   +-- Kabayan Switzerland  <- possible future experiment
```

The future names are examples of a possible family, not launched brands, purchased domains, roadmap commitments, or validated opportunities.

A second country should be explored only after Poland produces evidence for something useful and potentially repeatable. Country-specific law, institutions, procedures, language needs, alternatives, and user problems must be rediscovered rather than copied blindly.

## Market and language are separate dimensions

If another market eventually exists, do not model country and language as the same thing. For example, Germany may need Filipino, English, and German content. Poland may continue to have Filipino, English, and Polish content.

Do not implement this model today merely because it is conceptually true. It becomes an implementation concern when a real second market or another current requirement needs it.

## Implementation safety check

Build for Poland now. Avoid unnecessary Poland-only coupling, but do not pay an abstraction tax for hypothetical countries.

For a meaningful implementation decision, ask:

1. Is this the simplest good solution for the current Poland requirement?
2. Does it unnecessarily make a future second market difficult?
3. Are we adding an abstraction that has no concrete use today?

The preferred answers are **yes / no / no**.

Prefer simple, explicit, readable, reversible code over cleverness, speculative patterns, generic frameworks, provider hierarchies, multi-tenancy, or premature country abstractions. A naturally central brand/market value is better than scattering the same literal everywhere, but one value does not justify a configuration platform.

Generalize after a real second case appears or another concrete current requirement makes the abstraction useful.

## Boundary of the current decision

Choosing **Kabayan Poland** means the project can use a stable public identity while discovery continues. It does **not** mean:

- the product has been validated;
- `Kabayan` is exclusively owned by this project;
- trademark clearance has been completed;
- the domain or social handles are available until directly confirmed;
- Germany, Austria, Switzerland, or another market will be launched;
- the project should build multi-market architecture now.
